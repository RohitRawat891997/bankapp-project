1. Get the application endpoint:

   If you installed with ingress enabled and DNS pointing to your ingress controller:

     printf "Waiting for DNS/Ingress...\n"
     kubectl get ingress -l app.kubernetes.io/name=bankapp -n <namespace>

2. Check TLS certificate (cert-manager):

     kubectl get certificate -n <namespace>
     kubectl describe certificate <name> -n <namespace>

3. Useful checks:

   - Pods: kubectl get pods -l app.kubernetes.io/name=bankapp -n <namespace>
   - Service: kubectl get svc -l app.kubernetes.io/name=bankapp -n <namespace>
   - Logs: kubectl logs deploy/{{ include "bankapp.fullname" . }} -n <namespace>

4. To upgrade image:

   helm upgrade {{ .Release.Name }} ./helm/bankapp -n <namespace> --set app.image.tag=<new-tag>
